About Me

I'm a computational sociolinguist, and I am in the second year of my Linguistics PhD program at Northwestern University, working with Annette D'Onofrio and Rob Voigt. My main research interest is understanding how miscommunications arise between fluent speakers of the same language, and more specifically using multimodal interactional studies to explore variation in language use relating to neurotype (e.g. autistic or neurotypical interlocutors).

Before beginning my PhD program, I was a high school math teacher for four years, and then joined Northwestern, first for my Master's in Computer Science, and then as a researcher in Rob Voigt's Linguistic Mechanisms Lab studying racial disparities in news coverage of gun violence.
